# Claude Design brief: Epistemic Prism (`apart-epistemics`)

**Repo to import:** https://github.com/vikramwish/apart-epistemics  (Import, then GitHub, in Claude Design)
**What it is:** An interactive multi-agent epistemic simulation explorer that visualizes how monolithic, multi-lab, culturally distinct, and personalized AI assistants reshape topic diversity, source independence, accuracy, and confidence calibration.
**Who it's for:** AI epistemics researchers, alignment reviewers, and Apart Research hackathon judges who need to see the decoding-artifact dissociation (`T=0` vs `T=0.8`), calibration vs. diversity trade-off, and round-by-round belief collapse within 10 seconds.

## Aesthetic direction
- Direction: Editorial Quantitative Lab Notebook — warm archival paper `#FAF8F4`, crisp white specimen cards `#FFFFFF`, carbon ink `#171614`, muted warm border `#E2DDD3`, scientific vermilion accent `#C84B31`, deep cerulean `#245A7A`, botanical moss `#2F6F4E`, amber ochre `#B9770E`.
- References: Edward Tufte quantitative prints, *Distill.pub* interactive research essays, Oxford/Santa Fe Institute working paper monographs.
- Type: Editorial serif display (`Georgia`, `Newsreader`, `Instrument Serif` fallback stack) paired with clean humanist sans (`IBM Plex Sans`, `system-ui`) and monospace (`JetBrains Mono`, `ui-monospace`) for numerical telemetry.
- Avoid: generic SaaS look, purple-to-blue gradients on white, stock 3D blobs, emoji icons, Inter-on-white boilerplate.

## Screens and states
1. **Primary Explorer (`/`):** Top masthead with provenance badge (`synthetic` / `ollama` / `live`), 4-card key findings summary strip, interactive Condition Comparison Matrix & Epistemic Quadrant (Topic Diversity vs. Calibration Brier Score), `T=0 vs T=0.8` Decoding Double-Dissociation panel, Round Trajectory scrubber (`Round 0 -> 5`), and expandable Round Trace & Goldman Source-Overlap Inspector.
2. **Interactive States:** Track toggle (`All Tasks` / `Ground-Truth Calibration` / `Open-Ended Debate`), condition pill selection, round scrubber scrubbing trajectories live, and explicit empty/no-match state for filtered traces, loading skeleton state, and mobile responsive single-column stack at `390px`.
